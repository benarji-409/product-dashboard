Sales Dashboard (Power BI):
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard built using a retail sales dataset. The goal is to analyze business performance and uncover actionable insights.
Key Features:
1.Sales Overview
->Total revenue, average sales per transaction, and gross income.
->Branch-wise performance comparison with contribution to overall sales.
2.Customer Insights
->Purchase patterns of Members vs Normal customers.
->Gender-based buying behavior and average ratings.
3.Product Performance
->Revenue and quantity sold by product line.
->Identification of top-performing and low-performing product categories.
4.Time-based Analysis
->Sales trends across days and months.
->Peak sales hours to identify high-performing time slots.
Outcome:
This dashboard helps business stakeholders gain a clear 360° understanding of sales, customer behavior, and product performance. It enables data-driven decision-making to improve revenue, customer targeting, and resource allocation.
